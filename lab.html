09

washwish

Hold a key, speak, and it types
  1. Hold Ctrl + Win and talk. A liquid-metal orb reacts to your voice.
  2. whisper.cpp transcribes on your machine. No audio leaves it.
  3. The text is pasted wherever your cursor is, and your clipboard is put back.
Stack
Electron · TypeScript · whisper.cpp
Format
Windows tray app
or hold Space

The real orb renderer from washwish, running in your browser. It simulates a voice level; your mic is not used.

10

rilinote

A note board Claude can work on
  1. Note cards on an infinite canvas: linked, nested into subnotes, with checklists and priorities.
  2. An Express and WebSocket backend saves the board and pushes changes live to every open tab.
  3. Its own MCP server lets Claude create, edit and connect notes. There is no delete tool, on purpose.
Stack
React · Vite · Express · WebSocket · MCP
Format
Web app, deployable on Railway

Illustration of the MCP tools at work; not live board data.

Claude for After Effects

Describe the animation, watch the comp build
  1. A panel inside After Effects, wired through a small Node bridge to the Claude API.
  2. Claude reads the comp first, then plans its tool calls: layers before keyframes.
  3. It creates comps, layers, keyframes and effects, and the comp updates live.

12

Lirica Autotune

Real-time pitch correction
  1. A YIN tracker finds the pitch: a coarse pass at about 11 kHz, refined at full rate.
  2. The engine picks the target note from the scale or held MIDI notes, with Retune Speed, Flex-Tune and Humanize.
  3. A phase-locked PSOLA shifter resynthesises the voice, with formant control. Latency of 19 to 45 ms is reported to the host.
Stack
C++ · JUCE · Catch2
Format
VST3 + Standalone

A sketch of what Retune Speed does: 0 ms is the hard-tuned effect, 10 to 50 ms sounds natural.

13

JANE-01

A Juno-106-inspired polysynth
  1. Six voices (up to 16), built page by page from the original Juno-106 owner's manual.
  2. 24 dB ladder filter, HPF, chorus I, II and I+II, plus distortion, delay and reverb modelled on the software Jupiter-8.
  3. 128 patches in Group A/B, Bank and Patch, and a Windows installer for other PCs.
Stack
C++ · JUCE 8
Format
VST3 + Standalone, with installer

Panel sketch: move the faders to shape the filter curve. The plug-in has the full panel.

A drum instrument with its own groove library

  3. Drag any groove straight into an Ableton Live track as MIDI.
